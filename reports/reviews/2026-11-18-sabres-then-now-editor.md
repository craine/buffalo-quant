## Remaining polish, most important first

1. **The section 3 header misreads on its own.** "Power-play goals against: mostly bounces, both years" tells a fan the 21 is
   mostly luck. It isn't: those chances were worth 16.7, and the text says the shots got 30% more dangerous. The *jump* from 7 to
   21 is mostly bounces (last year's 7 was low, this year's 21 a bit high).
   - Replace "**3. Power-play goals against: mostly bounces, both years.**" with "**3. Power-play goals against: the jump from
     7 to 21 is mostly bounces.**"

2. **Section 2: Benson's sentence has two parentheticals in a row.**
   - Replace "The biggest gap is Zach Benson: 1 goal with a goalie in net (2 counting an empty-netter) on 80 shots that weren't
     blocked (including ones that missed the net), worth about 7.5 goals." with "The biggest gap is Zach Benson: 80 shots at a
     goalie that weren't blocked, worth about 7.5 goals, and 1 goal to show for it. (His other goal was into an empty net.)"
   - Quant: confirm Thompson's 13 has no empty-netters. If it does, use the same "with a goalie in net (N in all)" form there.

3. **Section 3: double colon.**
   - Replace "fewer shots, but better ones: the average power-play shot against has been about 30% more dangerous." with "fewer
     shots, but better ones. The average power-play shot against has been about 30% more dangerous."

4. **Move 3: "though not quite their usual form" is unclear.** A fan can't tell what "usual" means.
   - Writer: say what it compares to. For example, "...almost exactly what their chances were worth, a little below what the
     same players did in their other seasons." Only use that if the comparison is what the code measures. Otherwise cut the
     clause.

5. **Move 1 runs long, and "personnel" and "roster" mean the same thing.**
   - Replace "That's a coaching and personnel job as much as a roster one." with "That's as much a coaching job as a roster one."
   - Optional: move the parenthetical about 7.1 vs 6.3 shots and the penalty data to the end of the paragraph as its own short
     sentence. That keeps the main instruction ("fewer, and less dangerous, shots") as the last thing a host reads.

6. **Finishing bullet still reads like a stat.** "about 0.21 goals a game over the last 61 games"
   - Replace with "about 13 more goals than its chances were worth over the last 61 games". Use GF-xGF +13.04, which is already in
     the stat-heads section. That also makes "23 + 13 = about 36" visible to a fan.
