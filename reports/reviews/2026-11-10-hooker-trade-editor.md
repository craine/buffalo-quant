## Verdict: NEEDS EDITS (small, wording and order only; no new numbers)

The order is right: Hooker first, then Bishop, then what to watch. The headline names Hooker and says what he brings. Five problems:
1. "the partner Cole Bishop has been missing" says Hooker is the answer. That's more than we know.
2. The lead says "fills a real hole" and then sets the Poyer and Gardner-Johnson numbers side by side. Read that way, the partner caused the drop. It never says "tracked with."
3. The lead never says what this means for Bishop himself, which is half of what Charlie asked for.

5. The Bishop takeaway is buried below the method section.
