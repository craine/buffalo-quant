# Editor review (stage 3): 2026-wk12-kc-whos-left

**One sentence for radio:** "Twenty-two months after the AFC title game, Buffalo still has 8 of its 10 offensive regulars from that night, while both teams have rebuilt the secondary."

**Verdict: FAIL (needs edits).** The angle is sharp, and the lead, hero, card and headline agree. None of the phrases Charlie has rejected appear. The fixes are two overclaims, one repeat, and heavy lists.

## Must-fix

1. "The history between these teams is real, but only about half of the defenders who made it are still on the same side." -> "The history between these teams is real, but only about half of the defenders from that game are still on the same side."
   - Reason: "who made it" claims these defenders made the whole rivalry. The rivalry runs across four playoff meetings, and this story covers one game.
2. "Kansas City's secondary is mostly new since that game, and so is Buffalo's defensive staff." -> "Kansas City has replaced most of its secondary from that game, and Buffalo has a new defensive coordinator."
   - Reason: the second half overclaims (the Quant flagged it too), and "is mostly new since that game" is stilted.
3. "The sideline changed more than the roster." -> "The sideline changed too."
   - Reason: nothing measures the comparison, and the play-caller is the same.
4. Danna is said twice in a row. "Also gone from the front: Charles Omenihu (DE, now with Washington); Michael Danna (DE, now with Buffalo)." followed by "One of them crossed over: Michael Danna, who played 58% …" -> "Also gone from the front: Charles Omenihu (now with Washington) and Michael Danna, who crossed over. Danna played 58% of Kansas City's defensive snaps in that game and is now a Bill (1 game this season)."
5. Carry the Quant's Justin Reid fix into the body: "Justin Reid (SS, now with New Orleans)" -> "Justin Reid (released by New Orleans last week)".

## Should-fix: the "Gone:" lists

They are readable but heavy. Semicolons, positions and "no snaps on offense or defense this season" repeated four times read like a data dump. The chart already carries positions, so drop them from the body and use plain commas:

- "Gone: David Edwards (G, now with New Orleans); Mack Hollins (WR, now with New England)." -> "Gone: guard David Edwards (New Orleans) and receiver Mack Hollins (New England)."
- "Gone: A.J. Epenesa (DE, now with Philadelphia); DaQuan Jones (DT, now with New England); Kaiir Elam (CB, no snaps on offense or defense this season); Matt Milano (LB, no snaps on offense or defense this season); Rasul Douglas (CB, now with Washington); Taron Johnson (CB, now with Las Vegas)." -> "Gone: cornerbacks Rasul Douglas (Washington), Taron Johnson (Las Vegas) and Kaiir Elam (Chargers practice squad), plus A.J. Epenesa (Philadelphia), DaQuan Jones (New England) and Matt Milano, who hasn't played this season."
- "Gone: Jawaan Taylor (T, now with Atlanta); Joe Thuney (G, now with Chicago); JuJu Smith-Schuster (WR, no snaps on offense or defense this season); Kareem Hunt (RB, no snaps on offense or defense this season); Marquise Brown (WR, now with Philadelphia)." -> "Gone: linemen Jawaan Taylor (Atlanta) and Joe Thuney (Chicago), receiver Marquise Brown (Philadelphia), and JuJu Smith-Schuster and Kareem Hunt, who haven't played this season."
- "Bryan Cook (FS, now with Cincinnati); Jaylen Watson (CB, now with the Rams); Justin Reid (SS, now with New Orleans); Trent McDuffie (CB, now with the Rams)." -> "Trent McDuffie and Jaylen Watson are Rams, Bryan Cook is a Bengal, and Justin Reid was just released by New Orleans."

- The chart reads on its own: clear team headers, offense and defense blocks, and a plain-text status per player. The gray swatches have no legend, but the text column explains them, so that's fine. Bishop "CB" should read "S" (Quant item).

For Buffalo it's 71%:" -> "Players who were targeted in that title game get 35% of Kansas City's targets this season. For Buffalo it's 71%."
