   Teams that miss all three still win sometimes, and the story itself says better teams tend to do all three. Replace with
   "Sabres at Panthers: three keys for Buffalo in Florida" and "Three things go with winning, and they're our keys for Saturday."
2. "**Buffalo needs:**" (three times) -> "**What to watch:**". Same reason; it also reads like a template.
3. "But they're where this game will be decided." -> "But they're what to watch on Saturday."
4. "Buffalo is 6-6 in the 12 games it's done that this season, and 3-7 when it hasn't." A fan reads 6-6 as a record, but it hides overtime
   losses. -> "Buffalo has won 6 of the 12 games it's done that this season, and 3 of the 10 when it hasn't. Small samples, but the gap is there."
5. "That's the strangest number on the Sabres' season, and it's a small sample." Unsupported superlative. -> "Only Chicago and Vancouver have won
   a smaller share of the games they scored first, though 4 of Buffalo's other 8 went to overtime. It's a small sample." (writer computes)
