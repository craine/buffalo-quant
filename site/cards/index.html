Goalie cards

Every goalie who has played this season for the Sabres and the Golden Knights. Tap a card to flip it. Numbers through November 7, 2026; they update as the season goes.

How to read a card

Goals saved vs. an average goalie is the big number. Every shot has odds of going in, based on where it came from and what kind of shot it was. Add up the odds on every shot a goalie faced and you get how many goals an average goalie would have allowed. Subtract the goals he actually allowed. Positive means he stopped more than an average goalie would have.

Save rate is the share of shots on goal he stopped; the league's is 0.890 this season. Close-in save is the same for shots from within 20 feet, the hardest ones; the league's is 0.812.

The blended rating on the back answers "how good is he, really?" It adds this season's shots to half the weight of his past seasons' shots, plus 600 shots of league-average goaltending, so a dozen games can't swing it too far. As this season's shots pile up, they take over.

The source: the NHL's own play-by-play and our expected-goals model, the same one behind our Sabres stories. Cards marked small sample have fewer than 300 shots this season. Not affiliated with the NHL or any team; card designs are our own.
