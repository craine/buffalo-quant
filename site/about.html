About

Buffalo's quant desk.

Most sports coverage tells you what happened. We try to tell you what happens next, put a probability on it, and show our work.

Charlie Craine

Charlie grew up in Western New York and has been a Bills and Sabres fan his whole life. He has spent two decades building digital products and data teams, including nearly ten years as a CTO.

As Chief Digital Data Officer and CTO at Meister Media Worldwide, he turned a print publisher into a digital media company, growing digital revenue 350% and the audience from 250,000 to 6 million. At Amazon Web Services he was Global Head of Startups for Digital Customer Experience, across AWS's $6 billion startup business. Earlier he ran digital media at Future US in New York and at the Deseret News, and founded an entertainment site that grew to 5 million monthly page views.

He's a Kaggle Grandmaster, the top tier of the world's largest data science competition platform, and builds AI and machine-learning systems, including his own stock trading models. Buffalo Quant Desk uses the same approach on the Bills and Sabres: start from every play, measure what actually repeats, put a probability on what happens next, and keep score in public.

How we work

  • Every play, every shot. Everything starts from the leagues' own public play-by-play. Nothing is hand-entered.
  • Plain English. Every story explains where its numbers come from, with real examples, and saves the jargon for the end.
  • Receipts. Every prediction lives on the Ledger, logged before kickoff and graded in public.
  • Checked twice. Every story is recomputed by an independent review before it's published.

What we cover

  • Bills: game previews with logged predictions, post-game reports, and deep dives on players and coaches.
  • Sabres: built on our own expected-goals model, which measures how good a team's scoring chances really are.

What we don't do

Betting content. No odds, lines, picks or units. Projections are analysis, not wagering advice.
