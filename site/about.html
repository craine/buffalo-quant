About

Buffalo's quant desk.

Built by a data scientist and lifelong Buffalo fan, using the same methods behind systematic trading models and competitive machine learning.

Most sports coverage tells you what happened. We try to tell you what happens next, attach a probability to it, and show our work. Every number on this site is computed from public play-by-play data, with the method published alongside it. Every prediction lives on the Ledger, graded in public.

What we cover

  • Bills: weekly post-game reports, Thursday previews with logged calls, coaching and roster deep dives.
  • Sabres: coming this season, built on our own expected-goals model.

What we don't do

Betting content. No odds, lines, picks or units. Projections are analysis, not wagering advice.
