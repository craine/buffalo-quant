How grading works

  • Before kickoff or it doesn't count. The system rejects any call logged after the game starts.
  • Probabilities, not hot takes. A 70% call should hit about 70% of the time. Calibration is the score.
  • Brier score = (probability − outcome)², averaged. 0 is perfect, 0.25 is a coin flip.
  • Benchmarks are a naive baseline and public models. Never betting lines.
  • Append-only. Corrections are new rows that reference the old one. The full record is downloadable as CSV.
