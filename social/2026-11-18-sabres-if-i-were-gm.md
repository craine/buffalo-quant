## Instagram

If I were the Sabres' GM, I wouldn't sell.

After 21 games last season, the Sabres were 8-9-4. They went on to win 50. After 21 games this season: 8-9-4 again. Different team, though.

At full strength, five-on-five, Buffalo allows chances at a league-average rate. A man down, it allows the 4th-most in the NHL. That's the biggest single piece of the problem.

My plan:
1. Cut the chances allowed a man down.
2. Don't blow up the five-on-five lineup.
3. Be patient with cold shooters. They tend to come back to normal.
4. Leave the Sabres' own power play alone. It creates chances; the goals haven't gone in yet.
5. When I do shop, look for proven finishers.
6. Don't plan on the goalies to save it.

Last year's team was given a 12% playoff chance after 20 games and finished with 109 points. This year's was at 16% before Tuesday's loss in Philadelphia. Not done.
