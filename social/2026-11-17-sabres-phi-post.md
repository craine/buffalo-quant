## Instagram

Flyers 4, Sabres 3.

Jiri Kulich tied it 3-3 with 15:29 left. 52 seconds later, Christian Dvorak won it from a sharp angle, about 26 feet to the side of the middle of the net: a shot with about a 2% chance of going in.

It wasn't all bad luck. Every shot has a chance of going in based on where it was taken; add them up and Philadelphia's chances were worth 2.8 goals to Buffalo's 2.2. At five-on-five it was about even. The difference was the power play.

Our pregame call, logged before puck drop: a 48% chance the Sabres win. Philadelphia won, so it leaned the right way, barely.

Five straight losses now, with Washington, Florida and Tampa Bay still to go on the road trip.
